Business Requirements & Functional Overview
Version: 1.0
1. Project Overview
The objective of this project is to build an independent Liquidity Provider (LP) platform that enables 
Forex/CFD brokers to integrate with a single liquidity infrastructure without managing their own 
execution backend.
The platform acts as the central liquidity gateway between brokers and the execution infrastructure.
Brokers will integrate only with our LP platform through APIs. The underlying execution infrastructure 
remains completely hidden from brokers.
This platform is designed exclusively for B2B clients (Forex/CFD Brokers) and is not intended for retail 
traders.
2. Business Model
The platform operates as a B2B Liquidity Provider.
Business Flow:
Retail Trader
↓
Broker Platform
↓
Independent LP Platform
↓
Execution Infrastructure
↓
Liquidity Source
Retail traders never interact directly with the LP platform.

The broker remains the only entity responsible for client onboarding, client management, and client-
facing trading services.
The LP platform only provides liquidity, execution, reporting, and risk services to brokers.

5. Broker Integration
The broker integrates its own CRM, trading platform, or back-office system with the LP platform using 
REST APIs and WebSocket APIs.
All client orders are submitted to the LP platform.
The broker never communicates directly with the execution infrastructure.
The LP platform acts as the broker’s only trading gateway.
6. Market Data
The LP platform continuously streams real-time market prices.
Brokers may display these prices to their clients.
Brokers are not permitted to publish their own market data into the LP platform.
However, during order placement, if enabled by the Super Admin, the broker may submit an execution 
price together with the order request.
This execution price is used only for order validation and execution purposes.
It is not treated as a market data feed.
7. Execution Price Validation
Execution Price Validation is an optional feature controlled by the Super Admin.
When enabled, the broker may include an execution price with the order.
Before execution, the LP platform validates:
Symbol
Execution Price


=== PAGE 4 ===
Price Freshness
Allowed Price Deviation
Order Volume
Trading Session
If validation succeeds:
The submitted execution price is accepted.
If validation fails:
The platform follows the configured execution policy, which may include:
Rejecting the order
Executing using the LP market price
This prevents stale, manipulated, or invalid pricing.
8. Order Execution
Each broker is assigned one dedicated execution account.
This assignment is performed manually by the Super Admin.
Every order submitted by the broker is always executed through the broker’s assigned execution 
account.
The broker has no visibility into:
Execution Account
Execution Infrastructure
Liquidity Connections
Internal Trading Systems
The broker only receives the final execution result.
9. Pricing Model
The platform follows a Cost + Markup business model.
LP Platform provides:
Raw Spread
Raw Commission
Raw Swap


=== PAGE 5 ===
Broker configures:
Spread Markup
Client Commission
Pricing Profiles
The final trading conditions presented to the broker’s clients are generated from these configurations.
10. Revenue Model
Platform revenue is generated from the pricing difference between LP costs and broker pricing.
Example:
LP Raw Commission
↓
Broker Commission
↓
Broker Profit
Similarly:
LP Raw Spread
↓
Broker Spread Markup
↓
Final Client Spread
↓
Broker Revenue
The platform does not pay rebates to brokers under this business model.
Each broker is responsible for determining its own client pricing strategy within platform-defined limits.

15. Core Business Rules
The following business rules govern the platform:
Every broker operates independently.
Every broker has an individual wallet.
Every broker has an individual trading credit.
Every broker is assigned one dedicated execution account.
Brokers cannot view or access execution infrastructure.
Brokers cannot access other brokers’ data.
Client information is isolated per broker.
Super Admin controls all base pricing.
Brokers may only configure markup and commission within permitted limits.
Every trade must be recorded for reporting and auditing purposes.
All balances, positions, and PnL values are updated in real time.
